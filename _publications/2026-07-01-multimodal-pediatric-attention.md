**Authors**: Eleonora Montagnani, Matteo Giovanni Traverso, et al.

### Overview

This research focuses on the validation of a multimodal technological framework designed to assess pediatric visuospatial attention, aiming to overcome the limitations of the **traditional paper-based Modified Bells Test**. The study was conducted during my time as a Guest Student at the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT) in Genoa, within the Unit for Visually Impaired People (U-VIP), and formed the core of my Master's thesis research.

### Methodology and Technical Scope

* **Data Acquisition & Hardware**: Managed code development and data acquisition protocols involving typically developing children, utilizing **TechPAD** and a **Tobii Pro Spectrum** eye-tracker.
* **Data Processing & ML**: Performed statistical and machine learning (ML) analysis on the acquired behavioral and gaze data using **MATLAB** and **Python**.
* **Research Coordination**: Collaborated within a multidisciplinary team, actively participating in technical meetings to coordinate research efforts and optimize methodologies.
