Current Research
======
I recently completed my Master's Degree in Biomedical Engineering with a thesis developed as Guest Student at the **Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT) – U-VIP unit**, in Genoa. The project, *"Beyond paper and pencil: validation of a multimodal technological framework for evaluation of pediatric visuospatial attention"*, aimed to overcome the limitations of the traditional paper-based Modified Bells Test through a digital, multimodal assessment framework.

The work involved reprogramming a multisensorial prototype (TechPAD) and designing a digital protocol for a high-definition Tobii Pro Spectrum eye-tracker, alongside custom real-time data acquisition and statistical/machine learning analysis (supervised and unsupervised) in MATLAB and Python. Experimental sessions were conducted with 18 typically developing children (ages 4–12), under the supervision of Dr. Monica Gori and Dr. Eleonora Montagnani. A manuscript based on this work is currently in preparation.
