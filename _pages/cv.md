Experience
======
**Guest Researcher – Evaluation of pediatric visuospatial attention — IIT Unit for Visually Impaired People (U-VIP), Genoa (Nov 2025 – Jul 2026)**

Master's thesis research on validation of a multimodal framework to assess pediatric visuospatial attention, overcoming limitations of the traditional paper-based Modified Bells Test.

* Programmed the IIT TechPAD multisensory prototype and designed a digitized assessment environment similar to Modified Bells Test synchronized with a Tobii Pro Spectrum eye-tracker.
* Managed real-time data acquisition protocols and led on field experimental sessions with a cohort of 18 typically developing children (aged 4–12).
* Engineered custom pipelines in MATLAB and Python for gaze data processing, advanced statistical evaluation, and developed both supervised and unsupervised machine learning analysis to characterize visual and cognitive symptoms of fatigue.
* Collaborated within a highly specialized R&D team (supervised by Dr. M. Gori and Dr. E. Montagnani), actively defining project specifications and optimizing methodologies during technical meetings.

**Curricular Intern – Ultrasound and radiofrequency surgical devices — Ospedale Cottolengo, Turin (Sept 2022 – Dec 2022)**

Bachelor's degree internship analyzing and comparing two surgical devices: Harmonic 1100 (Ethicon, ultrasound) and LigaSure (Medtronic, radiofrequency).

* Attended live surgical procedures in the operating room to directly observe the instruments in action and evaluate their practical applications.
* Conducted structured interviews with head surgeons to collect qualitative feedback on device performance, handling, and adaptability across diverse intraoperative scenarios.
* Collaborated within a two-person team to execute the project, successfully managing strict deadlines while interfacing effectively with multiple hospital departments and medical staff.
